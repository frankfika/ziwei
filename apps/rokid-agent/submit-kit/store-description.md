## 一、中文版（直接粘贴进描述字段）

```
说出生辰，眼镜里立刻出现一张按传统规则算出的十二宫盘；抬头低头用准星点中哪一宫，按确认键，就听那一宫的解读。

【这是什么】
紫微星盘是一个跑在 Rokid AI Glasses 上的 AIUI 智能体，把紫微斗数这套传统术数的排盘规则做成一个能亲手操作的盘面工具：十二宫的宫位、主星、安星、五行局、大限，全部由确定性规则算出，不是模型生成的结果。盘面分本命、大限、流年、流月四层，同一张本命盘可循环切换，每层给出这一层的命宫落位与四化引动。解读文字来自公开解读数据集，按宫位检索原文，不自由发挥。

【怎么用】
1. 戴上眼镜，说出你的生辰，例如「1990年1月1日 子时 男」。
2. 盘面出现。中宫标出本次是端侧计算还是服务端计算，命宫自动选中。
3. 抬头、低头、转头，准星跟着视线在盘面格子里连续移动，落到哪一格就选中哪一格；左右键也能沿盘面外圈巡宫，十二宫都选得到。
4. 按上下键切换时间层：本命 → 大限 → 流年 → 流月，循环往复。中宫标题随层变化（如「流年 丙午 2026」），并标出这一层的命宫落位与四化；切层时眼镜会把这一层的标题念出来。
5. 按确认键，进入解读浮层：文字上屏，同时由语音朗读正文。
6. 在解读浮层里再按一次确认键，可以基于同一张盘追问。问「今年怎么样」自动带上流年那层的事实，问「大限呢」带上大限那层。

【核心技术特点】
· 排盘不经过模型：端侧内置约 860 KB 的单文件排盘引擎，断网也能出盘；同一生辰在任何设备上结果一致，可以复算复核。
· 四层运限同样在端侧算，与排盘共用同一份引擎。运限层给的是结构定位：这一层的命宫落在哪一宫、哪几个宫被四化引动。单色绿屏没有颜色可区分，四化因此带「限」「流」前缀，本命四化在运限层不显示。
· 解读不凭空生成：674,976 张盘面 × 13 个维度的解读文本，按 key 从公开数据集检索返回，模型只负责把原文组织成一段话，不参与排盘。
· 交互是指向不是点击：头部绝对朝向映射成连续二维准星，落格即选中；加速度计抬头切宫、镜腿按键作为兜底通道，任何时候都有可用的操作路径。
· 镜腿按键永远兜底：左右键沿盘面外圈巡宫（十二宫全覆盖，这一圈正好是十二宫序），上下键切时间层，确认键听解读或追问，返回键退出；端侧引擎没加载、压根没有运限层时，上下键退回纵向选宫。
· 许可合规已处理：数据集中引用他人原话的部分不在可商用范围内，服务端在返回前统一降级，界面标注当前文本形态；数据集本身不提供下载或镜像。
· 隐私：生辰与盘面只存在本机，上传的只有排盘所需的五个生辰参数；不申请摄像头、定位等权限。

【免责声明】
本智能体仅供传统文化学习与研究之用。盘面由确定性规则计算得出，可复算复核；解读文本来自公开的紫微斗数开源解读数据集原文检索。不代表任何预测或建议，不构成医疗、法律、投资或人生决策建议。

【数据集署名】
本作品使用了「紫微斗数开源解读数据集 v4.0」（674,976 张盘面 × 13 个维度）以及开源排盘引擎。来源：https://github.com/Renhuai123/ziwei-doushu ｜ 作者：王多鱼AI ｜ 数据集许可：CC BY 4.0；引擎代码：MIT。
```

## 三、英文版（直接粘贴进描述字段）

```
Say your birth date and time. Your glasses compute a twelve-palace Ziwei Doushu chart from deterministic traditional rules, then you look up and down to aim a crosshair at a palace and press the confirm key to hear that palace's interpretation read aloud.

WHAT IT IS
Ziwei Star Chart is an AIUI agent for Rokid AI Glasses. It turns the chart-casting rules of Ziwei Doushu - a traditional Chinese system of twelve-palace chart casting - into a chart you can operate by hand. The twelve palaces, their main stars, the star placements, the five-element frame and the decade limits are all computed by fixed rules. None of it is generated by a language model. The chart also has four layers - natal, decade, yearly and monthly - that you cycle through on the same chart; each layer shows where that layer's Life palace lands and which palaces the four transformations touch. The text of each interpretation is retrieved verbatim from a public dataset.

HOW TO USE
1. Put on the glasses and say your birth date and time, for example "January 1 1990, Zi hour, male".
2. The chart appears. The centre cell states whether it was computed on the device or on the server, and the Life palace is selected.
3. Look up, down and across. A crosshair follows your gaze across the chart cells; wherever it settles, that cell is the selected one. If you would rather not move your head, the left and right keys walk the outer ring of the chart and reach all twelve palaces.
4. Press the up and down keys to switch layer: natal, decade, yearly, monthly, and around again. The centre title changes with the layer (for example "流年 丙午 2026") and states that layer's Life palace placement and transformations; the glasses also read the layer title aloud.
5. Press the confirm key to open the interpretation. The text appears on screen and is read aloud.
6. Press the confirm key again inside the interpretation to ask a follow-up question about the same chart. Asking about "this year" brings in the yearly layer's facts; asking about the decade limit brings in the decade layer.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- No model in the calculation. A single-file charting engine of about 860 KB ships inside the app, so the chart works offline, and the same birth data always yields the same chart on any device. It can be recomputed and checked by hand.
- The four layers are computed on the device too, by that same engine. A layer gives structural placement only: where that layer's Life palace lands and which palaces the four transformations touch. The monochrome HUD cannot show colour, so transformations carry a "限" or "流" prefix, and natal transformations are hidden on the other layers.
- Nothing is invented. The interpretations come from a public dataset of 674,976 charts across 13 dimensions, retrieved by key. The model only organises retrieved text; it never casts the chart.
- Aiming, not tapping. Head orientation becomes a continuous two-dimensional crosshair; accelerometer tilt and the temple buttons serve as fallbacks.
- The temple buttons always work. Left and right walk the outer ring of the chart, which covers all twelve palaces and happens to be the traditional palace order; up and down switch the time layer; the confirm key plays the interpretation or takes a follow-up; the back key exits. Only when the on-device engine is not loaded - and there is then no time layer to switch to - do up and down fall back to moving between palaces.
- Licence handling. Parts of the dataset quote material that falls outside its commercial-use licence. The server redacts those passages before returning text, and the interface labels the current form of the text. The dataset itself is never redistributed.
- Privacy. Birth data and the chart stay on the device. Only the five birth parameters needed for retrieval are sent. No camera or location permission is requested.

DISCLAIMER
For cultural interest and study only. The chart is computed from deterministic rules and can be recomputed and verified; the interpretations are retrieved verbatim from a public open dataset. This is not a prediction or advice, and is not medical, legal or investment advice.

ATTRIBUTION
This project uses the "Ziwei Doushu Open Interpretation Dataset v4.0" (674,976 charts x 13 dimensions) and the open-source chart engine. Source: https://github.com/Renhuai123/ziwei-doushu Author: Wang Duoyu AI Dataset license: CC BY 4.0; engine code: MIT.
```

## 六、写这份文案时守的口径

1. **只写已实测确认的事实**：数据集规模（674,976 × 13 维 / 1950–2026 共 77 年）、引擎体积（约 860 KB）、硬件规格、交互链路，全部来自本地实测与代码。
2. **不写任何未验证状态**：眼镜真机尚未跑通全链路这件事不写进商店描述（那属于内部 checklist 与答辩准备），但也**不写**「已通过真机测试」「已上架」这类不实表述。
3. **不做结果承诺**：全篇只描述「怎么算出来的、文本从哪来」，不描述「能得到什么结果」。
4. **免责声明两处都要写**：应用内浮层已有一行（`pages/index/index.ink` 的 `overlay-note`），商店描述末尾再写一遍完整版。
5. **署名不可删改**：CC BY 4.0 的署名义务是硬要求，来源、作者、许可三项都不能省。
6. **与 `ATTRIBUTION.md` 的一处措辞差异**：署名里的「674,976 张盘面」与 `ATTRIBUTION.md` 里那个旧称指同一份数据，数字与来源、作者、许可均未改动，只按 `PROPOSAL.md` 9.3 的替换口径把旧称改成了「盘面」（英文版本来就是 `674,976 charts`）。若 Rokid 官方要求署名块与上游原文逐字一致，把这里改回 `ATTRIBUTION.md` 的写法即可，其余内容不受影响。
7. **「怎么用」这一段已与最终代码对齐**：现写的是「说生辰 → 出盘 → 准星选宫 → 上下键切时间层 → 确认键听解读 → 追问」。`lib/time-layer.js`（本命 / 大限 / 流年 / 流月 四层）**已接入** `pages/index/index.ink`：左右键沿盘面外圈巡宫（`lib/chart-layout.js` 的 `RING_ORDER`，十二宫全覆盖），上下键循环切层，确认键听解读（在解读浮层里再按一次进入追问），返回键退出。**眼镜端没有 tap / 双击处理**——全部交互走镜腿按键的 `onKeyUp`，所以本文案不出现「单击」「双击」这类说法。若代码侧再动按键，先改这里。
8. **运限层的口径是结构定位，不是运势**：运限层给出的是「这一层的命宫落在哪一宫、哪几个宫被四化引动」这类可复核的结构事实；十三维解读原文仍来自本命盘数据集的原文检索，运限层不重写文本。两者分工在 `faq.md` Q16 与 `video-script.md` S5 里都写明了，全篇不把运限层说成「运势」「转运」。
9. **运限层在端侧算**：与排盘共用同一份引擎；引擎没加载成功（走服务端兜底）时运限层整体不可用，界面退成只有本命盘。这是降级链的一条，不是缺陷描述——文案里不承诺「四层一定在」，也不必把这条写进商店描述。
