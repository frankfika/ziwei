## 一、中文版（直接粘贴进描述字段）

```
说出生辰，眼镜里立刻出现一张按传统规则算出的十二宫盘；抬头低头用准星点中哪一宫，就听那一宫的解读。

【这是什么】
紫微星盘是一个跑在 Rokid AI Glasses 上的 AIUI 智能体，把紫微斗数这套传统术数的排盘规则做成一个能亲手操作的盘面工具：十二宫的宫位、主星、安星、五行局、大限，全部由确定性规则算出，不是模型生成的结果。解读文字来自一份公开的解读数据集，按宫位检索原文，不自由发挥。

【怎么用】
1. 戴上眼镜，说出你的生辰，例如「1990年1月1日 子时 男」。
2. 盘面出现。中宫标出本次是端侧计算还是服务端计算，命宫自动选中。
3. 抬头、低头、转头，准星跟着视线在盘面格子里连续移动；每落到一格，眼镜念出这一宫的宫名与主星。
4. 单击确认，进入解读浮层：文字上屏，同时由语音朗读正文。
5. 再单击，可以基于同一张盘追问。

【核心技术特点】
· 排盘不经过模型：端侧内置约 860 KB 的单文件排盘引擎，断网也能出盘；同一生辰在任何设备上结果一致，可以复算复核。
· 解读不凭空生成：674,976 张盘面 × 13 个维度的解读文本，按 key 从公开数据集检索返回，模型只负责把原文组织成一段话，不参与排盘。
· 交互是指向不是点击：头部绝对朝向映射成连续二维准星，落格即选中；加速度计抬头切宫、镜腿按键作为兜底通道，任何时候都有可用的操作路径。
· 许可合规已处理：数据集中引用他人原话的部分不在可商用范围内，服务端在返回前统一降级，界面标注当前文本形态；数据集本身不提供下载或镜像。
· 隐私：生辰与盘面只存在本机，上传的只有排盘所需的五个生辰参数；不申请摄像头、定位等权限。

【免责声明】
本智能体仅供传统文化学习与研究之用。盘面由确定性规则计算得出，可复算复核；解读文本来自公开的紫微斗数开源解读数据集原文检索。不代表任何预测或建议，不构成医疗、法律、投资或人生决策建议。

【数据集署名】
本作品使用了「紫微斗数开源解读数据集 v4.0」（674,976 张盘面 × 13 个维度）以及开源排盘引擎。来源：https://github.com/Renhuai123/ziwei-doushu ｜ 作者：王多鱼AI ｜ 数据集许可：CC BY 4.0；引擎代码：MIT。
```

## 三、英文版（直接粘贴进描述字段）

```
Say your birth date and time. Your glasses compute a twelve-palace Ziwei Doushu chart from deterministic traditional rules, then you look up and down to aim a crosshair at a palace and hear that palace's interpretation read aloud.

WHAT IT IS
Ziwei Star Chart is an AIUI agent for Rokid AI Glasses. It turns the chart-casting rules of Ziwei Doushu - a traditional Chinese system of twelve-palace chart casting - into a chart you can operate by hand. The twelve palaces, their main stars, the star placements, the five-element frame and the decade limits are all computed by fixed rules. None of it is generated by a language model. The text of each interpretation is retrieved verbatim from a public dataset.

HOW TO USE
1. Put on the glasses and say your birth date and time, for example "January 1 1990, Zi hour, male".
2. The chart appears. The centre cell states whether it was computed on the device or on the server, and the Life palace is selected.
3. Look up, down and across. A crosshair follows your gaze across the chart cells; each time it settles on a cell, the glasses read out that palace's name and its main stars.
4. Press once to open the interpretation. The text appears on screen and is read aloud.
5. Press again to ask a follow-up question about the same chart.

TECHNICAL NOTES
- No model in the calculation. A single-file charting engine of about 860 KB ships inside the app, so the chart works offline, and the same birth data always yields the same chart on any device. It can be recomputed and checked by hand.
- Nothing is invented. The interpretations come from a public dataset of 674,976 charts across 13 dimensions, retrieved by key. The model only organises retrieved text; it never casts the chart.
- Aiming, not tapping. Head orientation becomes a continuous two-dimensional crosshair; accelerometer tilt and the temple buttons serve as fallbacks.
- Licence handling. Parts of the dataset quote material that falls outside its commercial-use licence. The server redacts those passages before returning text, and the interface labels the current form of the text. The dataset itself is never redistributed.
- Privacy. Birth data and the chart stay on the device. Only the five birth parameters needed for retrieval are sent. No camera or location permission is requested.

DISCLAIMER
For cultural interest and study only. The chart is computed from deterministic rules and can be recomputed and verified; the interpretations are retrieved verbatim from a public open dataset. This is not a prediction or advice, and is not medical, legal or investment advice.

ATTRIBUTION
This project uses the "Ziwei Doushu Open Interpretation Dataset v4.0" (674,976 charts x 13 dimensions) and the open-source chart engine. Source: https://github.com/Renhuai123/ziwei-doushu Author: Wang Duoyu AI Dataset license: CC BY 4.0; engine code: MIT.
```

## 六、写这份文案时守的口径

1. **只写已实测确认的事实**：数据集规模（674,976 × 13 维 / 1950–2026 共 77 年）、引擎体积（约 860 KB）、硬件规格、交互链路，全部来自本地实测与代码。
2. **不写任何未验证状态**：眼镜真机尚未跑通全链路这件事不写进商店描述（那属于内部 checklist 与答辩准备），但也**不写**「已通过真机测试」「已上架」这类不实表述。
3. **不做结果承诺**：全篇只描述「怎么算出来的、文本从哪来」，不描述「能得到什么结果」。
4. **免责声明两处都要写**：应用内浮层已有一行（`pages/index/index.ink` 的 `overlay-note`），商店描述末尾再写一遍完整版。
5. **署名不可删改**：CC BY 4.0 的署名义务是硬要求，来源、作者、许可三项都不能省。
6. **与 `ATTRIBUTION.md` 的一处措辞差异**：署名里的「674,976 张盘面」与 `ATTRIBUTION.md` 里那个旧称指同一份数据，数字与来源、作者、许可均未改动，只按 `PROPOSAL.md` 9.3 的替换口径把旧称改成了「盘面」（英文版本来就是 `674,976 charts`）。若 Rokid 官方要求署名块与上游原文逐字一致，把这里改回 `ATTRIBUTION.md` 的写法即可，其余内容不受影响。
